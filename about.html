About our school

Where every child
belongs.

Keelkari Convent School is a nurturing, co-educational learning community in Sadulshahar for students in Classes I–VIII.

Our purpose

A caring beginning makes all the difference.

We believe children flourish when they feel safe, seen and inspired. Our school combines structured learning with warmth, curiosity and the everyday habits that build character.

Through a strong educational foundation, we aim to help learners grow into responsible, confident and thoughtful young people.
