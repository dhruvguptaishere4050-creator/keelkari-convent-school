How information shared through the admissions form is intended to be handled.

Admissions form privacy

We collect only what is needed to respond.

What is collected

Parent or guardian name, contact details, interested class and the optional message you choose to send.

Why it is collected

To respond to your admissions enquiry, arrange a school visit where requested, and guide you through the admissions process.

Who can access it

Authorised members of the school administration team who handle admissions. Information should not be sold or used for unrelated marketing.

Your choice

You may ask the school office to correct or remove an enquiry record. Use the official school contact details once published on this website.
