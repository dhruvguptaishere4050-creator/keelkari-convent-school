Admissions help

Frequently asked questions.

Which classes does Keelkari Convent School offer?

Keelkari Convent School’s public profile lists Classes I–VIII.

How do I arrange a school visit?

Complete the enquiry form and include your preferred timing in the message. The school office can guide the next step.

When will I receive a response?

Response timing is managed by the school office. Make sure your mobile number is correct so the team can contact you.
